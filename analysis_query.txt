1. Two-way EDA business questions
A. Client × Amount
Which clients generate the highest total invoice value?

Which clients have the highest average invoice amount?

Which clients have the most invoices?

What is the minimum and maximum invoice amount for each client?

Are high-value invoices concentrated among a small number of clients?

Which clients contribute the largest percentage of total revenue?

Do repeat clients have higher average transaction values?

B. Client × Product
Which products are purchased by each client?

Which clients purchase the largest variety of products?

Which products are most frequently purchased by each client?

Are certain products strongly associated with particular clients?

Which clients are dependent on a single product?

Which products have the broadest client base?

C. Client × Bank Document
Which payment methods are preferred by each client?

Which clients primarily pay through bank transfers?

Which clients use cash deposits most frequently?

Are certain clients more likely to use bills of exchange?

What is the total amount collected through each payment method by client?

Do different clients have different payment behaviors?

D. Product × Amount
Which products generate the highest revenue?

Which products have the highest average invoice value?

Which products have the largest variation in transaction amounts?

Are certain products consistently associated with high-value invoices?

Which products contribute most to total sales value?

Which products have frequent transactions but relatively low average value?

E. Product × Bank Document
Which payment methods are most commonly used for each product?

Are expensive products more likely to be paid by bank transfer?

Are certain products disproportionately associated with cash deposits?

Which products have the highest amount collected through each payment method?

F. Invoice Date × Amount
How does invoice value change over time?

Which dates have unusually high-value invoices?

Are invoice amounts increasing or decreasing over time?

What is the daily total invoice value?

What is the average invoice amount by month?

Are there unusual spikes or drops in invoice value?

G. Invoice Date × Client
Which clients generate invoices most frequently over time?

Which clients are active during which periods?

Are some clients becoming more or less active over time?

Which dates have multiple invoices from the same client?

What is the monthly client contribution to revenue?

H. Invoice Date × Bank Document
How does payment-method usage change over time?

Which payment methods generate the highest value over time?

Are cash deposits becoming more or less frequent?

Are there particular periods with unusually high use of transfers or bills of exchange?

2. Three-way EDA business questions
Three-way analysis is useful when you want to understand how one relationship changes depending on a third variable.

A. Client × Product × Amount
Which client-product combinations generate the highest revenue?

Which clients spend the most on each product?

Which products generate the highest average invoice value for each client?

Are high-value transactions concentrated around particular client-product combinations?

Which client-product combinations have the most transactions?

Example:
TERRASTRUCT → CEMENT CPJ35 → €36,790

B. Client × Payment Method × Amount
Which clients generate the highest payment value through each bank-document type?

Does a client's preferred payment method change according to transaction value?

Which clients have the highest average transaction value for cash deposits?

Which clients have the highest average transaction value for transfers?

Are particular clients associated with high-value transactions through specific payment methods?

C. Product × Payment Method × Amount
Which products generate the most revenue through bank transfers?

Which products are most frequently paid for using cash deposits?

Which product-payment combinations have the highest average transaction value?

Are expensive products associated with particular payment methods?

Does payment behavior differ depending on the product purchased?

D. Date × Client × Amount
Which clients contributed the most revenue during each month?

Which clients had unusually large transactions on particular dates?

Are revenue spikes caused by existing major clients or new clients?

How does each client's average invoice value change over time?

Which clients show increasing transaction frequency over time?

E. Date × Product × Amount
Which products drive revenue growth over time?

Which products experience sudden increases in sales value?

Are certain products seasonal?

Which product has the highest monthly revenue?

Are high-value transactions concentrated in particular periods for specific products?

F. Date × Payment Method × Amount
How does the value collected through each payment method change over time?

Which payment method generates the highest monthly collection?

Are there periods where cash deposits become unusually high?

Are transfer payments associated with higher invoice values over time?

Are payment-method patterns changing from month to month?

3. More advanced three-way business questions
These are particularly useful for a management/business dashboard.

Revenue concentration
What percentage of total revenue comes from the top 5 client-product combinations?

Which clients generate high revenue across multiple products?

Which products generate high revenue across multiple clients?

Is revenue diversified or concentrated among a few client-product combinations?

Customer behavior
Which clients have both high transaction frequency and high average invoice value?

Which clients purchase multiple products but generate relatively low revenue?

Which clients consistently make high-value purchases through the same payment method?

Which clients have changing purchasing patterns over time?

Payment behavior
Which payment methods are associated with the largest transactions?

Which client-product combinations are most likely to use cash?

Which client-product combinations are most likely to use transfers?

Which payment method is responsible for the largest share of total collections?

Risk / anomaly analysis
Which client-product-payment combinations have unusually large amounts?

Are there unusual cash transactions compared with normal transaction values?

Are there dates where transaction values are significantly above the normal range?

Are duplicate client-product transactions occurring on the same date?

Are there clients with unusual payment methods compared with their historical behavior?

4. Particularly useful KPIs to derive
From your dataset, I'd calculate:

KPI	Business question
Total Revenue	How much has been invoiced?
Number of Invoices	How active is the business?
Average Invoice Value	What is the typical transaction size?
Median Invoice Value	What is the typical transaction without outlier influence?
Revenue per Client	Which clients contribute most?
Revenue per Product	Which products drive sales?
Invoice Count per Client	Which clients purchase most frequently?
Product Diversity per Client	How broadly does each client purchase?
Payment Method Share	How are invoices being paid?
Average Amount by Payment Method	Which payment method handles larger transactions?
Monthly Revenue	How does revenue evolve?
Monthly Invoice Count	How does transaction activity evolve?
Top Client-Product Combination	Where is revenue concentrated?
Top Client-Payment Combination	Who uses which payment mechanism?
Top Product-Payment Combination	How are products being paid for?
